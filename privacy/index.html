Privacy Policy

TABI – Commuter Alarm (Android app ph.tabi.app) · Last updated: [DATE OF PUBLISHING]

Sa madaling salita: Ginagamit ng TABI ang lokasyon mo para tumunog ang alarm bago ang babaan mo, kahit sarado ang app. Nananatili ito sa phone mo. Hindi namin ito ibinebenta, hindi ginagamit sa ads, at walang account na kailangan.

Who we are

TABI is made by Benjamin Baraquel Jr. in Cavite, Philippines ("we"). For any privacy question or request, email privacy@tabi.ph.

Location

TABI is an alarm that rings before you reach the stop you chose. To do that it needs your device's location, including in the background (while the app is closed or the phone is locked) during a trip you start. Before Android asks for background location, TABI explains why on its own screen.

  • Location is used only while a trip is running, to measure your distance and speed to your stop and ring the alarms.
  • Your location and your trips are processed and stored on your phone. We do not receive them, and we do not sell them or use them for advertising.
  • When the trip ends, TABI stops tracking.

What stays on your phone

Your saved and recent stops (name and coordinates), your settings, and your alarm history (the last 30 trips: times, distances, speed and whether an alarm was tapped). You can delete them in the app at any time (Ayusin), or all at once by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.

Maps and place search

To show the map and to search for places, the app contacts these services, which receive the map area being viewed or the words you type, plus your IP address, as any website does. They do not receive your GPS trip.

  • Map tiles: OpenFreeMap (OpenStreetMap data).
  • Place search: Photon (OpenStreetMap data).

Sharing a stop

If you choose to share a stop, TABI hands its name, coordinates and a map link to the app you pick (for example Messenger or Viber). Nothing is shared unless you tap Share.

Optional trip report

A future version may offer an optional trip report to help us improve alarm timing. It is off unless you choose to send it. If you do, it contains alarm timings, distances to the stop, speed, the trip outcome, your answer to "Tama ba ang timing?", battery level, phone model and Android version. It never contains your coordinates, stop names, name, email or exact trip time, and it is not linked to your identity. We will update this policy and the Play Store data safety section before it is released.

Payments

TABI is free today. If an optional Premium is offered later, payments are handled by Google Play; we do not receive your card details. We will update this policy before payments start.

Ads

TABI shows no ads today. If that changes, we will update this policy first, and ads will never appear on alarm or trip screens.

Children

TABI is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

Your rights

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) you have rights over your personal data, including to be informed, to access, to correct and to have it erased. Because TABI keeps your data on your phone, you can exercise most of these yourself in the app. For anything else, email privacy@tabi.ph.

Changes

If we change how TABI handles data, we will update this page and the date above, and tell users in the app or on our Facebook page when the change matters.
